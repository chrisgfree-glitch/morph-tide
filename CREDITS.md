# Texture credits

Terrain and hide maps are CC0 from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com). Sand, rock, grass, mud, lava, and moss are 1024px WebP. Reef and hide stay 512px. All keep mipmaps. Water shading is original code in this project. The guppy, steelhead, and sturgeon are original procedural meshes: no CC0 or CC-BY glTF of those three was both correctly proportioned and light enough for a phone. Their scale normals use the Leather026 set.
